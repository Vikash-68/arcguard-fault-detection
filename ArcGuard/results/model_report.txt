5. DIAGNOSTICS, DATA LEAKAGE & STRUCTURAL LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------
A. Data Leakage Assessment:
   - High-risk post-fault columns ('Duration of Fault (hrs)', 'Down time (hrs)',
     'Maintenance Status', 'Component Health') were strictly removed before training.
   - 'Fault ID' was removed to avoid memorizing arbitrary index numbers.
   - Preprocessing was strictly fit on training folds only.

B. Duplicate / Near-Duplicate Records:
   - Full-row duplicates: 0
   - Feature-subset duplicates: 0
   - No duplicated records inflated test performance.

C. Appropriateness of Random / Stratified Splitting:
   - The dataset contains 506 synthetic/tabular records without explicit time series timestamps.
   - Stratified random splitting preserves class ratios (approx 33% per class) across train and test.
   - Limitation: In actual power grids, faults occur sequentially over time across transmission feeder circuits.
     If time-series telemetry or specific feeder topology were available, a chronological (time-based) or
     substation-grouped split would be preferable to avoid temporal cross-contamination.

D. Disclaimers & Real-World Applicability:
   - The results do NOT prove real-world accuracy or partial-discharge detection capability.
   - Field deployments require continuous waveform sampling, transient electromagnetic wave capture,
     and acoustic/UHF sensor validation beyond basic tabular steady-state measurements.
